# Privacy Policy for Gravity Runner

Effective date: [Insert Date]

Gravity Runner is a mobile arcade game published by DMtools.fun LLC.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected or processed when you use Gravity Runner.

## Information We Collect

Gravity Runner does not require a user account and does not ask players to submit personal messages, contacts, or gameplay content.

The game may store basic gameplay progress locally on your device, such as:

- Best score
- Current level
- Difficulty setting
- Sound setting
- Retry progress
- Remove Ads state after confirmed purchase ownership

This local game data is used only to make the game work properly on your device.

## Ads

Gravity Runner may show ads through third-party advertising services. Ads may be shown on menu, pause, or game-over style screens. Ads are not intended to interrupt active gameplay.

Advertising providers may process device, app, and ad interaction information according to their own policies.

## Rewarded Ads

Gravity Runner may offer optional rewarded ads. These ads are only shown when a player chooses to watch an ad in exchange for a game benefit, such as extra retries.

## In-App Purchases

Gravity Runner may offer an optional one-time Remove Ads purchase. Purchases are processed through Samsung Galaxy Store / Samsung Checkout where applicable.

DMtools.fun LLC does not directly receive or store your full payment card details.

## Children

Gravity Runner is intended as a general audience arcade game. It is not designed to collect personal information from children.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Third-party services such as ad networks, app stores, payment processors, or analytics/crash reporting tools may process data according to their own policies.
